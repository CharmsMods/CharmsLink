Origin Point

Venge - Repo.

Hey, I'm Darynn, but I go by Charm? online. You might have seen me on YouTube playing games or making edits of those games.

My journey started when I discovered Venge.io, a niche browser shooter with a Discord community. I fell in love with the game and the people around it, and that is where a lot of my site work started.

Community Work

A Favorite Mod.

I contributed to the community by making a website for automatic Venge modding, where you could upload assets and textures, make them on the site, and export them as a mod compatible with both browser and client.

I have made several of my own mods, and Cream Mod became a community favorite for its style and performance. I was also recognized with Verification for my contribution to the game and community.

Now Playing

Passion.

After a while, I moved on from consistently playing Venge and keeping up with the modding site and community to playing other shooter games. A few that stand out are The FINALS and Banana Shooter.

Meanwhile, I have grown an online community through YouTube, Instagram, Discord, and more recently Twitch. I continue to improve my aim, upgrade my setup, and build visual tools that are free, high quality, and easy to trust.
